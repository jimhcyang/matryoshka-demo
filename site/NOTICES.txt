MATRYOSHKA PUBLIC PRESENTATION EXPORT

Publication scope: saved historical research examples and source-bound graph
exploration. It is not a live inference service or a claim of benchmark accuracy.

Original research code remains subject to the author's rights. No additional
open-source license or warranty is granted by this presentation export. Original
source quotations, names, dates and classification/handling markings are retained.

Central Intelligence Bulletins: historically released U.S. government documents,
available through the CIA Reading Room and the paired-release research corpus.
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/

Historical Iran and Vietnam diplomatic cables: previously publicly distributed
U.S. government cable texts used in the existing research corpus. Historical
classification labels are not altered. Public availability is not a claim that
this project has made a declassification decision.
https://wikileaks.org/plusd/

contains no private collaborator manuscripts or human-review comment exports.
