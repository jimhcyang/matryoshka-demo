Matryoshka | Evidence Field Guide
=================================

From a question to a source-bound decision
------------------------------------------
Start with the question. Finish with the quotation, not the rank.

Follow each question from its visible wording to the passages behind the local decision. The cases are saved, source-linked investigations: opening a result does not rerun inference. They were selected with knowledge of sources and outcomes, rather than sampled for a blind accuracy benchmark.

Visible question -> Grammar + requirements -> Eligible sources -> Retrieve + inspect -> Check + decide

Task: R / Redaction | What it asks: Propose text for one opaque gap. | What to inspect: Reading, literal source wording, completed sentence and support tier.
Task: M / Mosaic | What it asks: Assemble a source-cited account of a declarative statement. | What to inspect: Every required clause and any shared actor, event or relation.
Task: H / Hypothesis | What it asks: Test a declarative statement against the inspected evidence. | What to inspect: Positive evidence, signed opposition and unresolved requirements separately.

Two different uses of a model

A query-only planner can extract requirements and grammatical readings. That input is the public question, not the source origin or expected answer. The subsequent saved local execution retrieves sources, constructs proposals and applies evidence checks. An optional later analyst summary or review is advisory; it cannot rewrite the saved decision.

Read the outcome in three layers

Layer: Found | Meaning: A page or name was relevant enough to retrieve. This is not support.
Layer: Checked | Meaning: A bounded check actually inspected a page or candidate. Unchecked is unknown, not rejected.
Layer: Decided | Meaning: A saved source/grammar/coverage decision was recorded. Rank and a plausible name do not replace it.

Case choices may be informed by known source text and prior outcomes. Curation evidence and an independent review are not inference inputs or native achievements.

Grammar keeps the question intact
---------------------------------
A reading is a way the gap can function, not a guess at its contents.

Keep visible: Actor / action / object | Why it matters: A name elsewhere on a page cannot inherit another person's action.
Keep visible: Shared subjects | Why it matters: In 'A denied X and announced Y', both actions keep A. This uses visible grammar, not hidden identity.
Keep visible: Reporting + uncertainty | Why it matters: 'A said B might act' is a report of a possibility, not proof that B acted.
Keep visible: Negation + modifiers | Why it matters: Not, former, only, possessors, dates and attached qualifiers cannot be discarded to force a match.
Keep visible: All masks | Why it matters: The focal gap and neighboring masks remain opaque. Mask width and transport IDs supply no answer clues.

Redaction readings

The saved reading describes final inserted form, sentence function, possible target kind and number, with exact visible cues. An actor, possessor, example, or noun modifier is not interchangeable with the others. Unsupported or ambiguous readings can remain empty; the system need not try every form.

Source wording first; complete sentence next

Proposals come from source-bound spans. A literal proper-name prefix before an ordinary noun can nominate a compound modifier when the complete owned structure is retained. This does not license clipping a person's name, moving words across sentences, removing uncertainty or borrowing another owner's relation. A proposal still needs the unchanged final-form, head-kind, joint-slot and factual checks. A noun match can help context ordering without certifying the missing fact.

Nomination is not acceptance. A grammatical completion may remain contextual, partly grounded, withheld by a source-layout policy, or unsupported. New grammar does not create historical evidence or override a saved hold.

Mosaic and Hypothesis use independently checkable declarative requirements, not redaction readings. Conjunction does not by itself establish a causal link, same event or shared open identity across two different sources.

Search scores are not historical proof
--------------------------------------
Text ranking finds pages. Graphs organize and connect them. Quotations support claims.

Mechanism: BM25 | What it does: Ranks eligible text by word matches, with term-frequency and document-length adjustment. | What it does not establish: That the matched passage says the required fact.
Mechanism: RRF | What it does: Combines ranked lists. Each list contributes 1 / (60 + rank) in the inspected implementation. | What it does not establish: A correctness probability or a universal score across runs.
Mechanism: Hierarchy + diffusion | What it does: Spreads query influence over eligible nodes and hierarchy memberships, then helps rank source pages. | What it does not establish: A new historical relation between members of a group.
Mechanism: Direct source edge | What it does: A typed relation with bound endpoints, direction and an eligible quotation. | What it does not establish: An answer without actor, time, attribution and scope checks.

Which path am I seeing?

Path label: Saved retrieval walk | Honest interpretation: Use only when that run actually saved a directed walk with replayable witnesses.
Path label: Post-run inspection | Honest interpretation: A route assembled after execution to navigate selected evidence. Real node IDs and edge witnesses, but not a claim about the original search trajectory.
Path label: Hierarchy membership | Honest interpretation: Navigation between domain, theme, community and evidence; not a source assertion.
Path label: Source readout | Honest interpretation: A quotation endpoint. It is not itself a graph hop.

The default narrative hierarchy route is a fused retrieval kernel. It is not relabelled as a step-by-step directed tree. When no original walk was saved, node/edge paths are post-run selected-source inspection.

Visible bars may show rank position only. Diffusion mass means retrieval influence; overlapping incoming and restart contributions are not independent proofs. A drawing cap can hide nodes or edges that exist in the full graph. Absence from the canvas is not absence from retrieval or the corpus.

A shortlist is a bounded decision
---------------------------------
Being nominated, checked, displayed and recommended are different events.

Literal proposals -> Source families -> Check allocation -> Insertion + evidence -> Saved shortlist

Finite checks per reading balance primary sources and supplemental visible cues. Source-family diversity reduces repeated checks. The saved run records allocation, inspected pages and unassessed remainder; budgets and optional profiles can differ.

Redaction label: Evidence-backed suggestion | What can be concluded: Recorded focal source checks pass in the stated scope. Not proof of the hidden original word.
Redaction label: Partial grounding | What can be concluded: Some evidence is present; the complete missing assertion is not established.
Redaction label: Contextual suggestion | What can be concluded: A source-attested compatible lead, without focal factual support.
Redaction label: Held / not viable / unassessed | What can be concluded: Read the saved reason. A policy hold, incompatibility and lack of a check are distinct.

Mosaic and Hypothesis verdicts

Saved outcome: Complete / supported | Meaning: Every required clause is supported AND the required joint bindings and whole-query scope pass.
Saved outcome: Partial reconstruction / support / context | Meaning: There is evidence for part of the statement, but not a complete account.
Saved outcome: Opposed / partial opposition | Meaning: Eligible evidence directly contradicts all / some required clauses. Missing evidence is not opposition.
Saved outcome: Mixed / conflicted | Meaning: Different clauses have opposing signs, or the same clause has conflicting sources.
Saved outcome: Unresolved | Meaning: No sufficient signed result in the checked pool. Not a disproof and not a claim that no witness exists.

Coverage is not confidence. Even all clauses supported can leave an unbound shared actor/event or incomplete counter-scope check. Support, checked-pool coverage and recommendation stay separate.

Optional context fit describes saved overlap, relation, role, type and rank. Its authored weights are not probabilities and cannot override gates. Missing is not a mismatch. Case pages print saved values only; absent scores say 'Not recorded'.

Time and origins: what was allowed?
-----------------------------------
The evidence horizon is a source rule, not the date selected for a map drawing.

Ordinary case: Evidence date | Rule: Strictly earlier than the case cutoff. For source-assembled queries, the earliest contributing origin sets that cutoff.
Ordinary case: Origin exclusion | Rule: Every contributing origin document is excluded in full, not only the quoted passage. Companions cannot use their own SECRET births as CONF evidence.
Ordinary case: Iran + Vietnam | Rule: Permitted CONFIDENTIAL evidence only; whole-document NOFORN / NO FOREIGN DISSEM vetoes apply.
Ordinary case: CIB release | Rule: Explicit earlier/both visibility, permitted and not quarantined. Ordinary later-only rows cannot become earlier evidence.
Ordinary case: Citation | Rule: Document date, page identity, exact original span and page hash bind the saved quotation. A duplicate or repeated section is not an independent document.

A map's 'As of' control is inclusive for exploration. It never widens a case's strict earlier-source cutoff or the Swedish arm policy.

Use the workbench and all-map controls
--------------------------------------
Open a source to verify a statement; use the map to explore its neighborhood.

Workbench: Examples + corpus/task filters | Use: Choose a saved case; change the search method only among recorded conditions.
Workbench: Reading + candidate / requirement | Use: Compare alternatives, local support and gaps. Expand saved checks and score composition when available.
Workbench: Source quotation | Use: Inspect exact text, date and provenance. CIB paired physical-release PDFs appear where the catalog supplies them.
Workbench: Origin / reference unmask | Use: A separate reference view, never inference evidence. Respect any delayed-unmask and privacy controls.
Workbench: Guide in KG / Explore KG | Use: A case-bound inspection route versus independent full-map browsing. A disabled or unavailable guide has no fabricated substitute.

All-map control: Collection; Single / Pair | Use / limitation: Choose CIB, Iran or Vietnam; one projection or earlier/later comparison. Paired availability depends on the collection.
All-map control: As of; Date; - / +; Latest | Use / limitation: Set the inclusive exploration date. The earlier hierarchy stays fixed.
All-map control: Dim / Full / Hide | Use / limitation: Change how ineligible evidence is drawn, not its classification or case eligibility.
All-map control: Search + object type | Use / limitation: Search the full object index: entities, evidence, claims, events, relationships, hierarchy groups and opaque slots.
All-map control: Domains / Themes / Communities / Evidence | Use / limitation: Move through hierarchy levels. Back, Up and Neighborhood return, ascend or switch to direct neighbors where offered.
All-map control: Connected-only; drawing ceiling | Use / limitation: Limit search/display clutter; these are not inference budgets. Fit graph and Reset labels restore the view.
All-map control: Slots; Legend; provenance | Use / limitation: Opaque-slot markers navigate visible context, not hidden identities. Size is contained mass; thickness is relative support, not truth. Solid/dashed distinguish retained/later additions.
