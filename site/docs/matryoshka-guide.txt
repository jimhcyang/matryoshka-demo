Matryoshka: A Source-Linked Field Guide
These saved examples are curated historical demonstrations, not a blind benchmark or a claim that every case is solved. The workbench preserves the exact input, saved assessment, quotations and available source-ending graph walks.

Reading Evidence
Mosaic assembles facts; Hypothesis retains attributed assessments and opposition; Redaction proposes visible source-grounded fills. A high-ranked contextual candidate is not an evidence-backed recommendation. Grammar, graph navigation and overlapping names do not establish a historical action. Scores are deterministic evidence-and-fit indices, not probabilities. Different saved executions retain their own scoring contracts.

Sources and Originals
Evidence is dated on or before the input source day. CIB permits earlier or shared text, including eligible earlier-origin text. Iran and Vietnam require eligible confidential cables, handling guards and exclusion of the entire secret origin. Original input displays and later PDFs are inspection material, not automatically eligible evidence. Source cards preserve raw wording, ownership, attribution and uncertainty.

From Facts to Sources
Each input is separated into Facts with exact links to its original wording. Redaction also declares the grammatical role of the missing text. Fact searches nominate eligible documents and graph states. Graph connections expand discovery; hierarchy membership supplies navigation, not factual proof. Within an admitted document, each Fact can nominate its own best-matching page. Every inspected page is checked against every Fact, so one useful passage need not displace another. Displayed routes end at saved source quotations. Input origins, source quotations and extracted graph observations remain distinct and independently inspectable.

Decision Arithmetic
For a source at retrieval rank r, the relevance weight is 0.5 + 0.5 / (1 + log2(r)); an unrecorded rank receives 0.5. A contribution is that weight multiplied by the recorded fit. Only the largest contribution per document and direction is retained. Each Fact displays separate support, opposition and context totals; its signed index is support minus opposition. A complete decision additionally requires the source to establish the requested actor, action, arguments, attribution, polarity and qualifications. A large lexical score cannot repair a missing condition. Absence of evidence is not opposition, and repeated pages from one document do not become multiple independent votes.

Redaction Ranking
Candidates must pass their declared insertion checks and retain exact source wording or an explicitly recorded grammatical transformation. The main ranking combines evidence-tier points, source-function points and 35u/(1+u), where u = (support + fit + 0.5 context)/(1 + opposition). Fit includes source-local form checks, visible argument overlap and an equal balance of focal and best-background Fact overlap. Source-relative and adjunct suggestions use a separate contextual scale: 100 times (0.75 coverage + 0.25 Jaccard similarity), with document-frequency weighting where recorded. These contextual families do not certify the missing action or attribution. Charts preserve the executed scale and descending saved order, not a probability of correctness.

Reading a Source Account
A report that someone had to make a written statement can establish that the statement was made when the passage narrates the completed episode. A recommendation that someone should make it cannot. Likewise, reporting a possible negotiation result is not evidence that the result occurred. The workbench retains these distinctions in both the verdict and the highlighted source text. CIB sources offer earlier and later original PDF pages; cable sources retain their original numbered sections.
