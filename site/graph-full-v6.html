Reading the map

Hierarchy, topology, and provenance

01

Nested navigation

Domain, Theme, and Community are strict parent-child navigation partitions over the largest connected analyst projection. They are not a substitute for evidence edges.

02

Evidence topology

Entity, Event, and Claim are semantic nodes. Relations are edges supported by explicit extraction, audited associations, or tightly overlapping grounded evidence. The earlier-release hierarchy remains fixed when later-only evidence is added.

03

Visibility

Later-only evidence is derived by exact node and edge presence in the earlier projection. Dashed links encode that status; red emphasis is reserved for the object being inspected. Added evidence can enter the fixed earlier-release taxonomy but never refit it. This visibility distinction does not assert a classification level.
